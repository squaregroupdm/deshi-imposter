How to play
  1. One person hosts and shares the PIN or link. Friends join on their own phones. 3 to 12 players.
  2. Everyone peeks at their card. Crew see the secret word. The imposter only gets the category, the first letter and the length.
  3. Go round the table. Each person writes a one-word hint; it lands on the table for all to see.
  4. Tap a player to vote. Catch the imposter: crew score 1 each. Imposter survives or guesses the word: imposter scores 2. The host can switch on a timer in the settings.

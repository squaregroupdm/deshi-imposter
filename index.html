Deshi Imposter

Everyone gets the same secret word. One of you doesn't. Drop hints, read the room, vote them out.

or join one
How to play
  1. One person creates a room and shares the link. Everyone joins on their own phone. 3 to 12 players.
  2. Everyone secretly gets the same word (say, ফুচকা). The imposter only gets the category (Street Food).
  3. Take turns giving a one-word hint. Clear enough to prove you know the word, vague enough that the imposter can't guess it.
  4. Discuss in chat, then vote. Catch the imposter and every crew member scores 1. If the imposter survives the vote, or guesses the word after being caught, the imposter scores 2.
  5. Scores carry across rounds. The host can keep starting new rounds.

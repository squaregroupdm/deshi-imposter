Deshi Imposter

Everyone gets the same secret word. One of you doesn't. Hints on the table, then vote.

How to play
  1. One person hosts and shares the PIN or link. Friends join on their own phones. 3 to 12 players.
  2. Everyone peeks at their card. Crew see the secret word. The imposter only sees the category.
  3. Go round the table. Each person writes a one-word hint; it lands on the table for all to see.
  4. Tap a player to vote before the timer runs out. Catch the imposter: crew score 100 each. Imposter survives or guesses the word: imposter scores 200.
